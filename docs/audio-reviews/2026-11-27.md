# Audio review: Android v0.13.1, 2026-11-27

This is the first audio review. I can't hear the sounds; everything here is measured (loudness, peaks, loops, spectra,
coverage). How they sound is your call, so the listening questions at the end matter.

**Tools:** Python with numpy, scipy and pyloudnorm (installed for this review; ffmpeg isn't on the PC). The mix was
measured from a 28 s, 4-wizard fight recorded with Godot Movie Maker (`tools/shots/chaos.gd -- secs=28`, 48 kHz).

**Setup:**
- 24 WAV files, all 22.05 kHz mono, synthesized by `tools/build_audio.gd`. They're all ours, so no licences to track.
- Two buses: SFX at 0 dB and Music at -4 dB. **There are no effects on any bus** (no limiter).
- `scripts/sfx.gd` plays sounds through 12 shared voices in turn, with ±6% pitch jitter. World sounds fade out by
  900 px.
- Loops: lava and the two music tracks (loop mode forward).

## The mix in a fight

- **Clipping:**
  - Integrated -13.6 LUFS. Momentary is -17.6 / -14.1 / -11.0 LUFS (10th / 50th / 90th percentile), up to -8.5.
  - **True peak +0.3 dBTP and 202 clipped samples in 28 s.**
  - 17 of 280 windows (100 ms each) go over -1 dBFS. That's distortion on the phone during the busiest moments.
- **Low end:** 75% of the mix's energy is below 200 Hz
  ([spectrogram](2026-11-27-fight-mix-spectrogram.png): the bright band is the music's bass and the hits).
  Phone speakers play little of that. So the part you can't hear is what pushes the mix into clipping, and the
  part you can hear (200 Hz to 5 kHz) is thin: 20% of the energy at 200-2k, 3% at 2-5k.

## Top problems, ranked by how much they hurt the player

1. **The fight mix clips, and nothing catches it.**
   - What's wrong: the true peak reaches +0.3 dBTP, with 202 clipped samples in 28 s, and meteor_boom clips on
     its own.
   - Fix: add `AudioEffectHardLimiter` on Master (ceiling -1 dB) in `default_bus_layout.tres`. Rebuild
     meteor_boom 2 dB lower in `build_audio.gd`.
   - Size: small.
2. **Most of the energy is bass that phones can't play** (music 87-96% below 200 Hz; hit, meteor_boom and fight
   ~80%).
   - What's wrong: on the phone, the music will sound thin and far away, and the most common impact will be a
     click.
   - Fix: in `build_audio.gd`, add an octave-up (or saturated) layer at 200-1000 Hz to the music's bass line and
     to hit / meteor_boom / fight. High-pass everything at ~80 Hz, which also removes the small DC offsets. Then
     the Master limiter only has to deal with sound you can hear.
   - Size: medium.
3. **SFX levels are 12 dB apart for no reason.**
   - What's wrong: the staff (-27) and fireball (-23.7) are the sounds you make most, and they're the quietest.
     Lightning (-14.6) is 9 dB over the fireball. Death (-23.7) is quiet for the biggest event.
   - Fix: normalize in `build_audio.gd` by role:
     - main spell casts -18 LUFS ±1
     - impacts / hurt -17
     - death and kill -15
     - UI -21
     - ambience (lava) -24

     Then fine-tune by ear.
   - Size: small.
4. **Missing feedback sounds** (see the table).
   - Fix: add, in order of value:
     1. a bright "ting" for a shield reflect
     2. a short "kill" sting when you get credit for a kill
     3. a low rumble when the lava warning starts
     4. a slow heartbeat under 25% health
     5. a longer fanfare for the match
     6. a soft "denied" blip for not enough gold

     All can be synthesized in `build_audio.gd`.
   - Size: medium.
5. **Voices get stolen.**
   - What's wrong: 12 voices are shared round-robin, so in a chaotic fight the oldest sound is cut off, even a
     death or your own cast. There's no cap per sound, so `hit` spam can take every voice.
   - Fix: cap each sound at 3 at once (skip extras), give the player's own sounds and deaths priority, and steal
     the most-finished voice first.
   - Size: small.
6. **Lava loop.**
   - What's wrong: it's only 2 s long with a small step at the seam, so it likely ticks and repeats noticeably
     while you burn.
   - Fix: make it 6-8 s, crossfade the loop over 50 ms, and add random crackle pops.
   - Size: small.
7. **Music.**
   - What's wrong: the title track is 4.7 dB quieter than the fight track (a jump when you press Play), and there's
     a +6 dB step at the title loop point. Nothing changes for the shop or round end.
   - Fix: match both tracks to -16 LUFS. For the shop, a lighter variation of the music, or the fight music with
     a low-pass.
   - Size: small to medium.

## Listening questions for your phone
1. Can you hear the music at all on the phone speaker, especially the title? Does it sound thin?
2. In a chaotic fight, does it ever crackle or distort, e.g. when a meteor lands near other hits?
3. Can you hear your own fireball and staff swing during a busy fight? Do lightning or the homing orb feel too loud
   next to them?
4. When you burn in lava, is the sizzle tiring, or do you hear a tick every 2 seconds?
5. When you reflect a fireball with the shield, do you miss a sound there?
