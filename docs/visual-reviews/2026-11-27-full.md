# Visual review: the whole game (Android v0.13.1), 2026-11-27

This is the first review, so there are no earlier scores to compare against. It was judged from real frames: 5
scripted shots recorded at 1280x720, 30 fps, Compatibility renderer. The shots are in `tools/shots/` and can be
re-run with `--write-movie`:
- `walk8`: idle and 8 walk directions
- `spells`: all 12 spells cast at a dummy
- `edge_death`: a hit at the lava edge, the burn, and death on stone
- `chaos`: 4 AI wizards with shop spells
- `ui`: title, countdown and HUD, Far zoom, shop, pause, results

**What already works:**
- The lava edge (a dark crust line with an orange glow).
- The death in lava: the wizard glows, smokes and dissolves into embers.
- The Lightning bolt.
- The Shield ring.
- The Gravity Well spiral.
- The cooldown numbers on the buttons.
- The team colours at Close zoom.

## Top problems, ranked by how much they hurt the player

1. **Homing Orb looks like a fireball** (spells sheet, frames 240-276; the chaos sheet shows the same in the buttons).
   - What's wrong: it's a tinted fireball streak with the fireball's explosion, so you can't tell which one is
     coming at you. Your answer differs (a homing orb follows you, a fireball doesn't). The two shop buttons look
     alike too.
   - Fix: make it a real purple orb, round and pulsing, with a short purple trail, its own small purple burst on
     impact, and a distinct button icon. This can be drawn in Godot code like the other v0.12 effects, or made
     with PixelLab `vfx_workbench` ("small glowing violet orb, 16x16, 4-frame pulse, soft trail, top-down").
   - Size: small.
2. **Mixed pixel sizes in Meteor and Time Stop** ([pixel scale](2026-11-27-full/pixel_scale_mismatch.png)).
   - What's wrong: the meteor blast's pixels are about 2-3x a wizard's, and the Time Stop ring's about 4x. They
     look like they're from another game and break the pixel-art look the most.
   - Fix: redraw them at the wizards' scale (1 art px = 2 screen px at Close zoom), meaning more art pixels and no
     upscaling, in `tools/build_effects.gd`. Make the Time Stop ring a thin 1-2 px dashed ring with small clock
     ticks, not a thick band.
   - Size: medium.
3. **The back-facing sprites don't match** (walk sheet, frames 212-292).
   - What's wrong: the N and NE walk frames are a brighter, flatter orange-red with no hood detail or staff, and
     read as a different character. You see this every time you walk up the screen.
   - Fix: regenerate N and NE walk in PixelLab from the S idle as the reference, with the same palette and the
     staff in the hand. This fits the planned PixelLab finish (graphics step c), so add those two to that list.
   - Size: medium (about 2-4 generations).
4. **Blink and Swap barely show** (spells sheets, frames 144-156 and 342-372).
   - What's wrong: Blink leaves a 3 px dot, and Swap's trade of places reads as a camera glitch.
   - Fix: give Blink a short purple flash at both ends plus 3-4 fading after-images along the path. For Swap, add a
     bright line between the two wizards for 0.15 s and a puff at both spots. Godot code only.
   - Size: small.
5. **Readability at Far zoom and the small HUD text** (ui sheet, frames 220-258 and the HUD zoom).
   - What's wrong: at Far zoom the wizards are about 15 px tall. The scoreboard text is about 12 px, the countdown
     digit about 10 px, and the damage numbers are grey on grey.
   - Fix: raise the Far zoom (1.35 to about 1.6), scale the HUD font up 1.3x, make the countdown digit big under
     "Round N", and use white damage numbers with a dark outline (yellow when the player did the damage).
   - Size: small.
6. **The Meteor warning is too faint** (spells sheet, frames 396-414).
   - What's wrong: a pale red disc on grey. It's the one thing you must see in time.
   - Fix: a pulsing ring outline plus a darker shadow that grows as the meteor falls.
   - Size: small.
7. **The rocks and Rock Wall don't match the style** (spells sheet, frames 504-540; boulders in the HUD zoom).
   - What's wrong: they're smooth-shaded with no dark outline, and the Rock Wall reads as stacked loaves.
   - Fix: add a 1 px dark outline and 3-4 flat shade steps, and make the wall chunkier and more irregular.
     PixelLab `create_map_object` ("rough basalt boulder, top-down, dark outline") or Godot redraw.
   - Size: medium.
8. **UI font cohesion.**
   - What's wrong: a smooth vector sans everywhere, against pixel art.
   - Fix: a pixel font (e.g. a free 8x8 / 5x7 pixel font at whole-number scales) for titles, banners and buttons;
     keep the vector font only for long shop descriptions.
   - Size: medium.
9. **Smaller items:**
   - The very first frame of a game shows a full screen of lava before the camera snaps to the player (spells
     sheet, frame 0).
   - The far lava's tiling repeats visibly (edge-death sheet).
   - A reflected projectile looks exactly like the original. Tint it with the reflector's team colour.

## Could not judge from frames (for the phone check)
- Whether 30-fps recordings hide stutter: smoothness at 90 / 120 Hz on the phone.
- Whether the heat shimmer is distracting when moving.
- How big the HUD text really feels on your screen.
- Whether the Time Stop ellipse hides too much of the fight in real play.
- A lead, not a finding: in one artificial setup (bots placed about 2000 px off the island) the recording stopped
  updating after about 1.7 s. It doesn't happen in normal play or the other shots. Worth a look only if a frozen
  screen is ever seen on the phone.
